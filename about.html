About

Daniel Knowlton lives and writes in Frederick, MD. He holds an MFA in fiction from the University of Maryland and works at a language school in DC. He shares a home with his partner and several small beings, furry and otherwise. He promises to finish his novel-in-progress someday, and hopes you'll be patient. His characters have a long journey ahead of them.

Art on this website is courtesy of the wonderfully talented Michael Vachon, and you can see more of his work here.
