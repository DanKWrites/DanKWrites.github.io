We have all bred such brave little gods within our red, beating hearts.

Brave Little Gods is a literary science fiction novel-in-progress about a long, generational journey from this world to the next. On board The Ring, the middle generations have no experience of Earth and won't live to see their destination, but against all odds, they build a home and life that is all their own. Along the way, they face death, commune with ghosts, birth new life, and redefine what humanity looks like when it's free from the earth and must survive in the lifeless black.
